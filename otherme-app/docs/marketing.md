## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** *Create your character, drop them into any scene, animate it, and
talk to them live — all inside Nimiq Pay.*

**Elevator:** OtherMe turns a single photo or idea into a reusable AI character:
a full design sheet, cinematic scene images, 8-second video clips, and a live
voice conversation with a talking avatar. Pay as you go with NIM (+50% bonus) or
USDT — no subscriptions, no accounts to manage.

**Why it wins (talking points):** one identity across images/video/voice · runs
natively in a wallet (no app install) · crypto-native pay-per-use · bilingual
(EN/ES) · your creations are yours to share.

## 3. Campaign scripts (4)

All shot vertical (9:16), phone screen-recording inside Nimiq Pay, upbeat music,
on-screen captions (many watch muted). Keep each ≤30s except the flagship.

### A. "Why Other Me" — the flagship (45–60s)
Goal: explain the whole value in one watch. Landscape cut for YouTube too.
- **0–5s Hook:** *"What if you could create a person… and then actually talk to them?"* (fast montage: sheet → scene → video → talk)
- **5–20s Create:** upload a photo → AI fills the design sheet → generate the character image. Caption: *"One photo → a full character."*
- **20–32s Scene + Video:** put them in a scene, tap Animate → the 8s clip plays. Caption: *"Any scene. Then bring it to life."*
- **32–45s Talk:** open Talk, say hello, avatar replies in voice. Caption: *"Now have a real conversation."*
- **45–60s Close:** pricing flash (NIM +50%), logo, CTA *"Try it in Nimiq Pay → othermeapp.com."*

### B. "Hype Demos" — series (15–25s each)
Goal: fast dopamine, one wow per clip. Repeatable format.
- Templates: *"Photo → character in 10 seconds"* · *"I put my character in 5 wild scenes"* · *"Turning a still into an 8-second movie"* · *"Editing a video by just talking to it"* (show conversational edits).
- Structure: hook (0–2s) → result (2–20s, sped up) → CTA card. Caption pattern: *"Made entirely in a wallet. No app install."*

### C. "Show your Character" — UGC / community challenge (15–20s)
Goal: user-generated content engine. Prompt creators to make + share.
- On-screen: *"Show us your OtherMe 👇 Best character gets featured."*
- Mechanic: create a character → use in-app **Share** (auto-stamped
  "Created on OtherMeApp.com") → tag @OtherMeApp + #MyOtherMe.
- Team: feature the best weekly; seed with 5–10 diverse example characters.

### D. "Talk to Nimy" — mascot / signature demo (20–30s)
Goal: a memorable, ownable moment showcasing live voice.
- Concept: **Nimy**, a friendly Nimiq-themed OtherMe character (build once as the
  house mascot). Clip shows a real voice Q&A: *"Nimy, what's the best way to pay
  in OtherMe?"* → Nimy answers (NIM +50%). Playful, on-brand.
- Reusable: Nimy fronts announcements, tips, and replies to comments as clips.
