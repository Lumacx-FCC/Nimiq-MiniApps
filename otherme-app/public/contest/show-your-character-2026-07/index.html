1.The promotion

“Show your Character” is a creative promotion run by Other Me Labs, part of Studio Swai, San José, Costa Rica (“we”, “us”). It rewards people who create a character in Other Me and share it publicly.

This promotion is run and funded by Other Me alone. It is not run, sponsored, endorsed, or administered by Nimiq, nor by TikTok, X, YouTube, Instagram, Meta, or any other platform. See section 13.

These rules sit on top of the Other Me Terms & Conditions. Where the two conflict on a question about the app, credits, or your content, the Terms & Conditions govern; these rules govern the promotion itself.

2.Who can enter

You may enter if you:

  • are 18 or older at the time of entry;
  • are legally resident in a jurisdiction where this promotion is permitted; and
  • comply with the terms of the platform you post on.

You may not enter if you are an employee, contractor, or immediate family member of Other Me Labs or Studio Swai.

Void where prohibited. If your jurisdiction restricts promotions of this kind, or restricts receiving digital credits, you are not eligible and we may withdraw a prize awarded in error.

No purchase is necessary and buying credits does not improve your chance of winning. New accounts include free renders, which is enough to produce a valid entry.

3.Entry period

Entries open 30 July 2026 at 00:00 UTC and close 30 August 2026 at 23:59 UTC (the “entry period”).

Your post and your entry email must both be received within the entry period. Likes accrued after the closing time do not count (section 8).

4.How to enter

All five steps are required. An incomplete entry is not an entry.

  1. Follow the official Other Me account on the platform you are posting from (@OtherMeApp).
  2. Create your character at othermeapp.com.
  3. Open it inside Nimiq Pay — the character must be created in Other Me.
  4. Post it publicly with the hashtags #Nimiq #OtherMeApp #MiniAppsCompetition #AICharacter #AIvideo #AIart, tagging @OtherMeApp and @MiniAppsCompetition.
  5. Email us at info@othermeapp.com with the link to your post and the Nimiq Pay address you want any prize credited to (it starts with NQ).

One entry per person. You may post on more than one platform, but nominate a single post in your email — that post is your entry. If you nominate more than one, the first we receive counts. Multiple entries by the same person, or by coordinated accounts, void all of them (section 11).

Your post must remain public and unedited in substance until the results are published. A post that is deleted, hidden, or made private cannot be verified and will be disqualified.

5.What we ask you for — and what we never ask for

To pay a prize we need only two things: your post link and your Nimiq Pay address (the public address you receive with).

⚠️ We will never ask for your recovery words

We will never ask for your 24-word recovery phrase, your password, your private key, or remote access to your device — not by email, not by DM, not ever. Anyone who does is trying to steal from you, whoever they claim to be. A receiving address is all that is ever needed to send you anything.

6.Your entry content

By entering you confirm that your entry complies with sections 4, 5, and 11 of the Terms & Conditions. In particular:

  • you own or have the rights to the source image you used;
  • every identifiable person in it has consented to their likeness being used to generate an AI character;
  • it does not depict a minor;
  • it does not impersonate a real person or organisation, and is not sexual, hateful, harassing, defamatory, deceptive, or unlawful; and
  • it does not infringe anyone’s copyright or trademark.

We may disqualify any entry we consider in breach, or unsuitable to be associated with the brand, at our discretion. We do not pre-screen entries, and publishing or resharing an entry is not an endorsement of it.

7.Permission to show your entry

You keep ownership of your creation. By entering you grant us a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to display, reproduce, and reshare your entry post and the character image in it, in Other Me’s own marketing, for 24 months from the close of the entry period, with credit to your handle where practical.

You can withdraw this permission by emailing us; we will stop using it in new material, though we cannot recall material already published.

This licence covers your entry. It does not extend to your other creations, and it does not let us sell your character.

8.How likes are counted

  • Only likes on the single nominated public post count. Views, shares, comments, saves, and reposts do not.
  • The count is taken from the number publicly visible on the platform at the close of the entry period, 30 August 2026 at 23:59 UTC.
  • We record a screenshot and timestamp of each entry’s count at close. Where a platform’s count is unavailable, ambiguous, or clearly manipulated, we may disregard the entry.
  • Our count at close is final. Later changes on the platform — including likes gained afterwards, or removed by the platform — do not change a result.
  • Results will be published by 7 September 2026 and winners contacted at the email they entered from.

9.Prizes

Your score is 10 credits per qualifying like, capped by the tier you reach:

TierWho qualifiesAward
1The first entry to reach 100 likes1,000 credits
2The top 50 entries by like countup to 250 credits
3Every valid entry with 10 or more likesup to 100 credits

Prizes are not cumulative. You receive the single highest amount you qualify for, not the sum of the tiers.

  • Ties are broken by the earlier post timestamp.
  • The total prize pool is capped at 20,000 credits. If valid entries would exceed the cap, tiers are paid in order — Tier 1, then Tier 2, then Tier 3 in descending like order — until the pool is exhausted. Entries beyond that point receive nothing.
  • If fewer than 50 valid entries qualify, only the qualifying entries are paid. If no entry reaches 100 likes, Tier 1 is not awarded.
  • Prizes are not exchangeable for cash, NIM, USDT, or anything else, and there is no alternative prize.

What a credit is

Credits are a prepaid access balance inside Other Me used to generate characters, scenes, clips, and conversation. As stated in section 3 of the Terms & Conditions, they are not money, not a deposit, and not a token: they carry no monetary value, cannot be redeemed for cash, cannot be transferred between accounts, and are not a financial product.

10.How prizes are paid — read this before you enter

Prize credits are added to the Other Me account belonging to the Nimiq wallet address you give us, by 30 September 2026.

You must sign in with that same Nimiq wallet to see them

Other Me currently keeps a separate, unlinked account for each sign-in method (Terms & Conditions section 2). Credits sent to a wallet address are visible only when you sign in with that wallet — typically by opening Other Me inside Nimiq Pay. If you normally sign in by email, your prize will not appear in that email account, and we cannot merge the two.

Give us the address of the wallet you actually use with Other Me.

  • We pay to the address you supply. An address you mistype is not recoverable and we cannot reverse a grant.
  • If an address is invalid, or we cannot reach you by 23 September 2026, the prize is forfeited — we need time before the 30 September payout date to correct a bad address.
  • Credits granted as a prize are subject to the same terms as purchased credits, except that they were not purchased: they are non-refundable and carry no consumer right of withdrawal, since no payment was made.
  • Any tax on a prize is the winner’s own responsibility.

11.Disqualification

We may disqualify an entry, or all entries from a person, and reclaim credits already granted, where we reasonably believe there has been:

  • purchased, botted, incentivised, or exchanged likes — including engagement pods, follow-for-like rings, and paid promotion of the post to inflate likes;
  • use of multiple, duplicate, or coordinated accounts;
  • a post that is deleted, hidden, made private, or materially edited before results are published;
  • an entry that is not a genuine Other Me creation;
  • a breach of section 6, or of the Terms & Conditions; or
  • any attempt to interfere with the operation or fairness of the promotion.

Organic sharing — asking friends, your community, or your followers to look at your post — is fine and expected. Paying for likes is not.

Our determination is final. We are not obliged to explain a disqualification in detail, and doing so in one case creates no obligation in another.

12.Your data

To run the promotion we process your email address, your post link and public handle, and the Nimiq wallet address you supply.

  • We use it only to verify entries, pay prizes, and publish results.
  • Wallet addresses and transaction records are handled as described in sections 6 and 10 of the Terms & Conditions.
  • We keep entry records for 12 months after the close, then delete them, except where we must keep them longer for tax or legal reasons.
  • We will not add you to a marketing list because you entered. If we ask whether you would like updates, saying no has no effect on your entry.
  • Winners’ public handles will be published with the results. Your email address and wallet address will not be published.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email info@othermeapp.com. A deletion request before prizes are paid will end your participation, since we would no longer be able to pay you.
